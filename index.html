CLOUD-AS-OUROBOROS

The Post-Digital Infinite Scroll

Curated by Alan Perry, Snow Chen, and the Algorithm

Pavilion of the 7th Wrong Biennale

Even beneath the unblinking eyes of surveillance capitalism, whose luminosity hungers for every trace of being, sanctuaries of shadow remain.

Within this pavilion, artists turn toward magick as compass and critical lens, to tap into a language of code or charm in order to navigate the post-digital cosmos.

In defiance of technofeudal lords, we gather to dream in circuitry, to move unseen, and to reclaim the ineffable from data's devouring glare.

Conjuring content from the digital ether...
